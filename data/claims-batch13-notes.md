## Per-theory sources and claims

### 114 — Friston's free-energy principle
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/friston-s-free-energy-principle-and-active-inference
  (title verified: "Friston's Free-Energy Principle and Active Inference";
  CTT category: Materialism / Homeostatic & Affective)
- **c263 "Consciousness minimizes surprise"** (→ c47): the signature move —
  consciousness as the natural outcome of minimizing the difference between
  perceptual expectations and real-time sensory inputs via internal generative
  models. Entails c47 ("The brain is a prediction engine") because the page
  describes exactly that machinery: "internal models that infer causes of
  sensations to reduce prediction errors."
- **c264 "Consciousness is a process"** (standalone): Friston's deflationary
  frame — "consciousness is nothing more and nothing less than a natural
  process such as evolution or the weather"; not a thing to be defined but a
  process to be understood. Kept standalone (an anti-reification claim, not a
  mechanistic one).
- Theory lists ["c263", "c264"]; inherits c47.

### 115 — Seth's beast machine
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/seth-s-beast-machine-theory
  (title verified: "Seth's 'Beast Machine' Theory";
  CTT category: Materialism / Homeostatic & Affective)
- **c265 "Experience is controlled hallucination"** (→ c48): Seth's signature
  framing — we never experience sensory signals themselves, only top-down
  interpretations; contents of consciousness are a "waking dream… reined in
  by reality." Entails c48 ("Experience is controlled prediction") — it IS a
  controlled prediction, but more specific: the radical point that perception
  is top-down fantasy, not a transparent window. Deliberately kept distinct
  from (not a reuse of) c48 so the hallucinatory framing is captured.
- **c266 "Consciousness keeps the body alive"** (→ c110): the beast-machine
  root — prediction machinery arose from the fundamental biological imperative
  to regulate bodily physiology; we experience world and self "with, through,
  and because of" our living bodies. Entails c110 ("Homeostatic regulation is
  the point") — a more specific version of it (predictive machinery for
  physiology, not just feelings reporting internal state).
- Theory lists ["c265", "c266"]; inherits c48, c47, c110.

### 116 — Godfrey-Smith's gradualist evolution
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/godfrey-smith-s-naturalist-gradualist-diversified-evolution-of-consciousness
  (title verified; CTT category: Materialism / Phylogenetic/Evolutionary)
- **c267 "Consciousness evolved gradually"** (standalone): rejects threshold /
  on-off models; subjectivity emerged in degrees over deep time, from the
  sensing–acting–self-monitoring linkage in animals. Deliberately NOT c163
  ("Life is sentient from start") — he anchors subjectivity in animal nervous
  systems, not single cells. Deliberately not c74 (Aurobindo's involved
  consciousness) despite the "gradual" language — his is Darwinian, not
  metaphysical.
- **c268 "Experience diversified across animals"** (→ c267): "What evolved is
  not the same thing repeatedly, but something more divergent in kind" —
  octopus vs mammal as independent evolutionary experiments; sensory and
  evaluative strands can diverge. Entails c267 (diversified gradual evolution).
- Theory lists ["c268"]; inherits c267.

### 117 — Ginsburg–Jablonka unlimited associative learning
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/ginsburg-and-jablonka-s-associative-learning-during-evolution
  (title verified; CTT category: Materialism / Phylogenetic/Evolutionary)
- **c269 "Learning capacity marks consciousness"** (standalone): the
  evolutionary-transition-marker methodology — "unlimited associative learning"
  (ascribing motivational value to novel compound stimuli, usable for future
  learning) is the observable marker of the transition to minimal
  consciousness. Stated as their positive methodological claim.
- **c270 "Value systems drive the transition"** (→ c269): the engine of the
  transition is evaluation — learning fused with an integrated value system
  (good/bad for the organism) turns association into meaning. Entails c269.
- Theory lists ["c270"]; inherits c269.
- Distinguished from Godfrey-Smith 116: G–J are transition-marker theorists
  (a specific learning capacity marks the boundary); G–S is a
  gradualist/diversification theorist (rejects single-criterion litmus tests).

### 118 — Husserl's transcendental phenomenology
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/husserl-s-transcendental-phenomenology
  (title verified; CTT category: Phenomenology — used as the family label)
- **c271 "All consciousness is about something"** (standalone): intentionality —
  every conscious act (perceiving, judging, imagining, remembering, doubting)
  points beyond itself to an object-as-meant. Kept purely descriptive, per the
  task's instruction not to dress phenomenological description as mechanism.
- **c272 "The world can be bracketed"** (standalone): the epoché as rigorous
  first-person method — suspend the natural attitude and describe how things
  show up (givenness, time, embodiment, intersubjectivity). Positive rendering
  of a methodological claim.
- Theory lists ["c271", "c272"]. Caveat recorded: phenomenology brackets
  metaphysics, so no c0 entailment and no stance for/against materialism.
- Family "Phenomenology" is a NEW family label — the CTT landscape itself
  files Husserl and Merleau-Ponty under a top-level "Phenomenology" category
  (like Materialism), so this is landscape-accurate, not invented. (Batch 6
  introduced "Quantum" the same way.)

### 119 — Merleau-Ponty's lived body
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/merleau-pontys-embodied-perception-and-the-lived-body
  (title verified: "Merleau-Ponty's Embodied Perception and the Lived Body";

  corrected at pre-merge review to satisfy the most-specific rule).
- Theory inherits c273, c112. Caveat recorded: no c0 entailment; MP rejects

- Overlap note: c273/c274 sit near Noë's c262 (consciousness as skilled

### 120 — Hoel's causal emergence
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/hoel-s-causal-emergence
  (title verified; CTT category: Information — used as the family label)
- **c275 "Macro can out-cause micro"** (standalone): causal emergence as a
  general, measurable phenomenon — a macro-scale description can be more
  causally informative than the most detailed micro description. Deliberately
  NOT c40 (top-down causation): c40 is specifically about higher levels

  the level where causal emergence peaks, where the brain has maximal control
  over its own future. Entails c275.
- **c277 "Stories have causal power"** (→ c276): the narrative mind — stories
  are not epiphenomenal but real causal scaffolding. Most specific claim;
  theory lists ["c277"] only.

## Judgment calls / merge-review flags

1. **Seth c265 vs c48.** c265 entails rather than reuses c48: the
   "controlled hallucination" framing is Seth's distinctive radicalization
   (perception as waking dream). If a reviewer thinks it's identical to c48,
   collapsing is easy.
2. **Friston c264.** The "process, not a thing" deflationism is standalone
   and could look like a meta-claim rather than a content claim. Kept because
   it is Friston's stated distinctive position on the page and it does real
   quiz work (separates process views from thing views).
3. **Godfrey-Smith vs Ginsburg–Jablonka.** Both are evolutionary
   gradualists, but G–S (c268) is about diversification and rejecting litmus
   tests, while G–J (c270) are transition-marker theorists with a specific
   criterion. If they converge in quiz use, a shared "evolution is gradual"
   parent could be introduced.
4. **New "Phenomenology" family.** Introduced for Husserl and Merleau-Ponty
   because CTT files them under a top-level Phenomenology category and
   "Materialism" would be actively wrong (Husserl brackets metaphysics; MP
   rejects reductive materialism). The color-coding in the app may need a
   color for this family — flag for merge.
5. **Noë (113) vs Merleau-Ponty (119).** Both live near c112/bodily-skill
   territory; Noë's c262 → c49 (extended-mind lineage) while MP's c273 →
   c112 (phenomenological lineage). Kept distinct deliberately.
6. **Hoel c275 vs c40.** c275 (macro causal-informativeness) does not entail
   c40 (top-down causation) — the former is a claim about causal capacity at
   scales, the latter about conscious states pushing neurons around.
   Related but distinct; flagged for possible future merge.
7. **Hoel narrative claim.** c277 (stories have causal power) sits under
   c276 — the "narrative mind" is Hoel's, and no existing claim covers
   narrative-structured consciousness. New chain, no forced reuse.
8. **Corrections made pre-merge (draft phase):** c264's short label exceeded
   6 words ("Consciousness is a process, not a thing" → "Consciousness is a
   process"); theory 119 listed both c273 and c274 in violation of the
   most-specific rule → now lists ["c274"] only.
