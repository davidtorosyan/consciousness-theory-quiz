## Per-theory sources and claims

### 34 — Schopenhauer's world as Will
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/schopenhauer-s-the-world-as-will
- **c130 "The world is representation"** — "the world is my representation":
  objects exist only for a subject, structured by space, time, causality.
  Entails **c126** (Kant's "we know only things-as-experienced") — Schopenhauer
  takes over Kant's distinction as his starting point.
- **c131 "Will is the inner essence"** — the inner essence of all phenomena,
  organic and inorganic, is will: "not conscious, rational purpose, but a
  mindless, insatiable striving." Standalone. Deliberately does NOT entail c1:
  the will is explicitly non-conscious, so "everything is mind" would be false
  for Schopenhauer.
- **c132 "Consciousness is the will's tool"** — intellect is a secondary
  biological instrument the will produces; consciousness "arises only at higher
  grades" and can be extinguished while the will persists. Entails c131.
- **c133 "We know will from inside"** — the body known externally as physical
  object and immediately from within as willing: the single window onto the
  thing-in-itself. Entails c131.
- Theory lists ["c132", "c133", "c130"] (most-specific; c131, c126 inherited).
- Family: Idealism, with caveat — the page itself stresses he is "not a
  panpsychist about consciousness" and demotes consciousness rather than
  making it fundamental. Filed by structural analogy; the caveat says so.

### 35 — Yogācāra mind-only
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/yog-c-ras-mind-only-how-store-consciousness-makes-experience-cohere-without-external-objects
- **c134 "Store consciousness underlies experience"** — ālayavijñāna retains
  seeds (bīja) / impressions (vāsanā) and conditions the arising of ordinary
  awareness; the river analogy; ceases at liberation so it is not a smuggled-in
  self. Standalone.
- **c135 "Subject and object are constructed"** — the grāhaka-grāhya
  (apprehender-apprehended) bifurcation occurs within cognition, not as a
  division in reality. Entails **c1** (everything is fundamentally
  consciousness or mind).
- Theory lists ["c134", "c135"] (c1 inherited).
- Caveat: whether Yogācāra is ontological or epistemological idealism is the
  central interpretive dispute (Trivedi, Kellner/Taber); the c1 inheritance
  reflects the stronger ontological reading.
- Judgment call: "Mind-only" does NOT reuse Berkeley's c122 ("Only minds and
  ideas exist") — Vasubandhu argues objects have no explanatory work left
  (seeds do), but the tradition's own texts hedge, and conflating a Buddhist
  no-self flow with Berkeleyan theistic immaterialism would flatten the
  difference.

### 36 — Nāgārjuna's emptiness
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/n-g-rjuna-s-empty-consciousness-experience-without-essence
- **c136 "Everything exists only dependently"** — framed positively: nothing has
  svabhāva (existence from its own side); everything exists only in dependence
  on causes, parts, relations, concepts. Consciousness included without
  exception. Standalone.
- **c137 "Consciousness real, yet empty"** — conventionally real (seeing,
  feeling, suffering occur) but no ultimate essence, no self-grounding core, no
  enduring experiencer; "not nihilism." Entails c136.
- **c138 "No self-illuminating core"** — awareness is not self-intimating; no
  privileged reflexive access (MMK 3; Vigrahavyāvartanī); bears on modern
  self-representational and higher-order theories. Entails c137.
- Theory lists ["c138"] (c137, c136 inherited).
- Caveat: filed Idealism by structural analogy only — the CTT assessment
  explicitly refuses both illusionism and idealism for Madhyamaka ("each for
  positing an intrinsic nature, one denied to experience and one attributed to
  it"). Note the negative phrasing of c137/c138: precedent exists (c53, c67).
- Judgment call: this theory is near the boundary of the "positive claims only"
  starting point; Nāgārjuna offers no positive account by method, so the claims
  are framed as positively as the material allows (dependent existence first).

### 37 — McGilchrist's relational creative-process idealism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/mcgilchrist-s-relational-creative-process-idealism
- **c139 "Consciousness is primordial process"** — "irreducible, primordial and
  omnipresent… not a thing; it is a creative process"; "the fundamental given
  natural fact." Entails **c9** (experience is fundamental).
- **c140 "Matter is a phase of consciousness"** — the ice/water analogy: matter
  is "a special case, or a phase of consciousness… a kind of consciousness —
  for a time." Entails **c1**.
- **c141 "Relations are primary, things secondary"** — "everything is
  relational… relata are secondary to relationship"; subjects and objects
  co-arise, "no view from nowhere." Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c139", "c140", "c141"] (c9, c1 inherited).
- Judgment call: c139/c140 do NOT reuse c28 ("conscious cosmos first") or c3
  ("One cosmic mind") — McGilchrist stresses process-not-thing, so the
  cosmic-mind claims would mischaracterize him. c141 vs Yogācāra's c135
  (subject-object constructed): related but distinct traditions (process
  relationality vs cognition-only); kept separate.

### 38 — T. Campbell's Big TOE
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/campbell-s-theory-of-everything
- **c142 "Reality is a computed virtuality"** — the physical universe is a
  virtual reality computed by the Larger Consciousness System; "There is no
  objective world outside of us"; the hard problem is dissolved by dropping
  belief in a fundamental external reality. Standalone — deliberately NOT
  entailing c5 ("Perception is an interface," a Hoffman-style evolutionary
  interface claim) and NOT entailing c14 ("Simulation is not consciousness"):
  Campbell's simulation IS the reality, and its metaphysics is not Hoffman's.
- **c143 "We are consciousness units evolving"** — we are "Individuated Units
  of Consciousness: immortal, interconnected parts of a Larger Consciousness
  System," playing the VR game to evolve consciousness quality "from fear to
  love"; AUO → AUM (the One became the Many). Entails **c28** (universe as a
  whole is conscious; individual minds derive from it).
- Theory lists ["c142", "c143"] (c28 inherited).
- Caveat: Campbell "Reject[s] Dualism, Materialism and Idealism" — the
  idealism label is refused by the author; filed Idealism by structural
  analogy.

### 39 — Goswami's self-aware universe
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/goswami-s-self-aware-universe
- **c144 "Consciousness collapses the wave"** — consciousness "is the agency
  that collapses the wave of a quantum object… making it an immanent particle
  in the world of manifestation"; the observer's role made metaphysical.
  Standalone.
- **c145 "One consciousness, we participate"** — "there is only one
  consciousness in the universe, one subject of experience, in which we all
  (somehow) participate"; "the ego… is constricted consciousness." Entails
  **c35** (one universal consciousness; individuals are delimitations of it).
- Theory lists ["c144", "c145"] (c35, c1 inherited).
- Family: Idealism — fits without caveat (explicitly "consciousness, not
  matter, is the primary 'stuff' of creation").

### 40 — Builes's modal idealism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/builes-modal-idealism
- **c146 "Idealism is metaphysically necessary"** — "it is metaphysically
  necessary that Idealism is true": every fundamental entity is conscious, every
  fundamental property phenomenal. Entails **c1**.
- **c147 "Possible worlds are divine thoughts"** — only one world is
  fundamental; "All possible worlds exist, but only one world is fundamental.
  The other possible worlds exist as thoughts in the mind of a divine being."
  Entails c146.
- Theory lists ["c147"] (c146, c1 inherited).
- Caveat: the CTT entry presents the ideal observer as divine, while the quiz
  summary says "non-theistic and stark: no God needed" — Builes's argument
  requires an ideal observer, "divine or structurally similar"; the claim text
  reflects that disjunction, and the caveat notes the CTT page's theistic
  framing.

### 61 — A. Harris's fundamental-field panpsychism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/a-harris-s-panpsychism-as-fundamental-field
- **c148 "Consciousness is a fundamental field"** — "think of consciousness like
  spacetime — a fundamental field that's everywhere"; intrinsic to matter "all
  along." Entails **c25** (intrinsic nature is (proto-)conscious) — Harris
  prefers the terms "intrinsic nature theory" / "intrinsic field theory."
- **c149 "Field without selfhood"** — rocks don't have experiences or "points
  of view"; "The solution to the combination problem is that there is really
  no 'combining' going on at all with respect to consciousness itself." Entails
  c148.
- Theory lists ["c149"] (c148, c25 inherited).
- Caveat: Harris "is split between a brain-based explanation and a panpsychic
  one" and is "not convinced that panpsychism offers the correct answer" — she
  presents it as a valid category, not a conviction. The claim records the
  position as formulated, not endorsement.
- Family: Panpsychism.

### 62 — Tye's irreducible consciousness*
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/tye-s-irreducible-consciousness
- **c150 "Consciousness* is irreducible"** — at the most fundamental level
  'consciousness*' ("what others would call protoconsciousness") is
  irreducible: "I don't see any plausible (non-arbitrary) reductive physical
  candidates." Entails **c34** (consciousness irreducible). The asterisk marks
  Tye's own technical term; the plain line glosses it.
- **c151 "Consciousness is on/off"** — "there are no borderline cases of
  phenomenal consciousness itself: it's an on/off matter," and "the best
  explanation for the absence of borderline cases… is that phenomenal
  consciousness is irreducible." Entails c150.
- **c152 "Complex minds from functional combinations"** — "consciousness* gets
  transferred from the fundamental entities to complex combinations… that play
  the right functional role and that represent various properties"; "what I have
  now is my original PANIC theory with the addition of consciousness*" — his
  combination-problem solution. Entails c150.
- Theory lists ["c151", "c152"] (c150, c34 inherited).
- Judgment call: c151 (on/off) vs c46-ish qualia nuances — kept separate; this
  is a metaphysical sharpness claim about consciousness itself, not about its
  contents.
- Family: Panpsychism.

### 63 — Mørch's phenomenal-powers panpsychism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/morch-s-phenomenal-causal-powers-panpsychism
- **c153 "Phenomenal properties have causal powers"** — "Phenomenal qualities
  are not inert; they are essentially powerful — they are the very things that
  make matter behave as it does"; "categorical bases of certain causal powers."
  Entails **c25** (Russellian inspiration: consciousness is the intrinsic
  hardware the physical structure runs on). Deliberately does NOT entail c40
  ("Top-down causation is real"): Mørch's claim is an identity claim
  (phenomenal properties = causal powers), not an emergent top-down claim.
- Theory lists ["c153"] (c25 inherited).
- Note: c153 vs c67 ("Consciousness does nothing", epiphenomenalism) is a
  genuine contradiction pair — permitted under the alignment-not-consistency
  model.
- Family: Panpsychism.
