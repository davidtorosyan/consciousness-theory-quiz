## Per-theory sources and claims

### 41 — Aurobindo's evolving consciousness
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/sri-aurobindo-s-evolving-consciousness
- **c74 "Evolution unfolds involved consciousness"** — from the involution doctrine:
  "Nothing can evolve which is not already involved"; evolution is the unfolding
  of consciousness previously concealed in matter. Entails **c9** (experience is
  fundamental): the doctrine only makes sense if consciousness is already there
  at the bottom.
- **c75 "Matter is involved spirit"** — "Matter not as separate from Spirit, but
  as Spirit in its most primitive and involved form"; no ontological gap between
  matter and spirit. Entails **c1** (everything is fundamentally consciousness).
- **c76 "Consciousness evolves ever higher"** — human mind "much too imperfect
  ... to be the final resting point"; higher supramental consciousness will
  develop. Entails **c74** (a specific instance of the unfolding).
- Theory lists ["c76", "c75"] (most-specific; c74 and c9/c1 inherited).
- Family: Idealism — the page calls it "evolutionary idealism" / "integral
  non-dualism" / "spiritual monism". Blurb: "Consciousness is the fundamental
  reality, evolving from matter toward the divine."
- Judgment call: Aurobindo's "divine life on Earth" / supramental teleology is
  captured by c76's directionality; the specifically theological content (God,
  theogenesis-style fulfillment) is deliberately left as flavor rather than a
  separate claim — it is soteriology/eschatology, not a consciousness claim.

### 48 — Nyāya contingent-consciousness self
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/ny-ya-s-contingent-consciousness-of-the-enduring-self
- **c77 "Self can exist unconsciously"** — signature move: consciousness is an
  adventitious quality (āgantuka guṇa) of an eternal, intrinsically non-conscious
  self; the self persists through dreamless sleep and liberation without
  experience. Entails **c6** (the self is a nonphysical substance).
- **c78 "Awareness needs a second act"** — cognitions reveal objects but never
  themselves; awareness of awareness requires a distinct subsequent
  "apperception" (anuvyavasāya). Standalone — deliberately NOT entailing c45
  (higher-order thought): for Nyāya first-order cognitions are phenomenal
  without the second act; the second act is for introspective *knowledge*, not
  for consciousness itself. Entailing c45 would misrepresent the view.
- **c79 "Qualities are in objects"** — direct realism: color/sound/taste inhere
  in mind-independent objects; "redness was never inside." Standalone.
- Reused **c7** ("Self can outlive the body"): Nyāya's ātman is eternal and
  survives in liberation with all qualities ceased — a genuine affirmation.
- Theory lists ["c77", "c78", "c79", "c7"]; c6 inherited via c77 and c7.
- Family: Dualism — the page's own classification ("substance dualism without
  essential consciousness").
- Judgment call: the liberation-as-extinction-of-experience material is folded
  into c77's plain/text rather than a separate claim; the Buddhist/Mīmāṃsā
  debate context is background, not claims.

### 49 — Libet's conscious mental field
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/libets-conscious-mental-field
- **c80 "Experience is a nonphysical field"** — the CMF: produced by billions
  of neurons, spatially coextensive, yet belonging to no known physical category
  and not detectable by physical means. Entails new **c81** (see below).
- **c81 "Nonphysical, not a substance"** — Libet *explicitly denied* Cartesian
  substance: the CMF is a system property, not something that could exist apart
  from the brain. This new broader claim exists so Libet's nonphysicalism does
  not drag in c6's substance language. Standalone root.
- **c82 "Field influences neurons both ways"** — generated by neurons, acts
  back on them (the conscious veto). Entails **c81** — deliberately NOT c8,
  because c8 entails c6 (nonphysical substance), which Libet rejected. This
  mirrors the batch-4 occasionalism handling.
- Theory lists ["c80", "c82"] (most-specific; c81 inherited).
- Family: Dualism — the page: "Landscape therefore has good reason to classify
  the mature CMF within Dualisms."
- Judgment call: the isolated-slab testability and the readiness-potential
  irony are biographical/methodological, not consciousness claims — omitted.

### 50 — Beck–Eccles quantum synapse interactionism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/beck-eccles-s-quantum-processes-in-the-synapse
- **c83 "Mind steers quantum synapses"** — "the mental intention (the volition)
  becomes neurally effective by momentarily increasing the probability of
  exocytosis in selected cortical areas" via quantum state reduction. Entails
  **c8** (mind can affect the brain) — Eccles was a substance dualist, so
  c8 → c6 is correct here (unlike Libet).
- **c84 "Quantum gaps leave room"** — the load-bearing premise: quantum
  indeterminacy means the brain's future isn't fully fixed, opening the doorway
  for mental influence. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c83", "c84"].
- Family: Dualism.
- Judgment call: kept to the two load-bearing claims; Stapp's supporting gloss
  adds no distinct claim.

### 55 — Velmans's reflexive monism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/velmans-s-reflexive-monism
- Reused **c19** ("one substance, two aspects"): the page explicitly calls it
  "a dual-aspect theory (in the tradition of Spinoza)" — a clean fit.
- **c85 "Experience is where it seems"** — phenomenological externalism: "Insofar
  as experiences are anywhere, they are roughly where they seem to be"; the
  pain is in the foot, not the brain. Standalone.
- **c86 "Universe aware of itself"** — the "reflexive" half: the universe
  differentiates into parts and becomes conscious of itself through them.
  Entails **c19** (a specific elaboration of the one-reality view).
- Theory lists ["c85", "c86"]; c19 inherited via c86 (and would be inherited
  anyway as the listed theory's own root — listed directly nowhere, correctly).
- Family: Neutral monism (dual-aspect).
- Judgment call: "the brain is what the mind looks like from the outside" is
  folded into c85's framing rather than a separate claim.

### 56 — Pauli–Jung psychophysical complementarity
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/pauli-s-one-world-two-aspects-psychophysical-complementarity
- **c87 "Mind-matter complementarity"** — mind and matter as complementary
  aspects (Bohr's sense: mutually exclusive yet jointly necessary) of the
  unus mundus. Entails **c19** — complementarity is a specific kind of
  dual-aspect monism.
- **c88 "Neutral ground unknowable"** — the unus mundus is "the condition of
  knowledge rather than an object of it"; neither pole has ontological
  priority. This is what distinguishes Pauli–Jung from Jamesian/Russellian
  neutral monism (whose neutral bases are treated as accessible building
  blocks). Standalone.
- **c89 "Awareness alters its object"** — becoming conscious of an unconscious
  content irreversibly transforms it, as measurement disturbs a quantum
  system. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c87", "c88", "c89"]; c19 inherited via c87.
- Family: Neutral monism.
- Judgment call: archetypes-as-ordering-operators and synchronicity are
  left out — they are mechanisms/corollaries of the framework, and
  synchronicity in particular is empirically contested metaphysics rather
  than a crisp consciousness claim. Refinement candidate if the family grows.

### 57 — Teilhard de Chardin's evolving consciousness
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/teilhard-de-chardin-s-evolving-consciousness
- Reused **c19** directly: the page states "Teilhard was a dual-aspect monist"
  — matter and consciousness as two aspects of the same cosmic stuff.
- **c90 "Complexity means consciousness"** — the law of complexity-consciousness:
  greater exterior complexity → greater interior consciousness; "evolution is
  the rise of consciousness." Standalone (a Russellian monist could affirm a
  version without the dual-aspect framing, so no entailment to c19).
- **c91 "Evolution drawn to Omega"** — Omega as prime mover and absolute whole,
  drawing evolution toward convergence; identified with God. Standalone.
- **c92 "Planetary mind-layer forming"** — the noosphere. Entails **c90** (a
  specific instance of the complexity-consciousness rise).
- Theory lists ["c19", "c91", "c92"]; c90 inherited via c92.
- Family: Neutral monism (page's explicit classification).
- Judgment call: listing c19 directly (a very general claim) is correct here —
  nothing the theory lists is more specific than it except via c92 → c90, and
  Teilhard genuinely affirms plain dual-aspect monism.

### 58 — Davidson's anomalous monism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/davidson-s-anomalous-monism
- The famous triad becomes three claims:
- **c93 "Mental events are physical"** — token identity: each mental event is
  numerically identical with a physical event. Standalone — deliberately NOT
  entailing c0 ("entirely explainable by ordinary physics"): Davidson's
  anomalism denies strict psychophysical laws, so c0's explainability claim is
  in genuine tension with the view.
- **c94 "No mind-brain laws"** — mental anomalism: no strict laws connect the
  mental and physical vocabularies. Standalone.
- **c95 "Mind causes physical events"** — the first premise of the triad:
  mental events have genuine causal powers. Standalone — deliberately NOT c8,
  because c8 entails c6 (a nonphysical mind); Davidson is an event-monist.
  (Same pattern as batch-4 occasionalism and batch-5 Libet.)
- Theory lists ["c93", "c94", "c95"].
- Family: Materialism — judgment call. The page notes the view is "frequently
  classed as a property dualism" and "belongs equally to Non-Reductive
  Physicalism," but its ontology is token-physical (each mental event IS a
  physical event), consistent with id 16 (non-reductive physicalism) sitting in
  Materialism. Flagged as a possible refinement.
- Judgment call: the deductive argument structure (the triad's apparent
  inconsistency resolved by token identity) is background reasoning, not
  additional claims.

### 59 — Pereira's triple-aspect monism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/pereira-s-three-aspect-monism
- **c96 "Three aspects, one substance"** — physiological, mental-unconscious
  (informational patterns), mental-conscious (felt experience): three
  inseparable aspects of one substance. Standalone root.
- **c97 "Consciousness co-emerges"** — consciousness as a co-emergent property
  of the three aspects together. Entails **c96**.
- **c98 "Astrocytes make feelings"** — the distinctive neurobiology: astrocyte
  networks integrate distributed information into large waves that instantiate
  feelings, feeding back on synapses. Entails **c96**.
- Deliberately NOT reused **c66** ("Three irreducible domains"): c66 is
  trialist — three *irreducible* domains. Pereira's aspects are inseparable
  aspects of one substance, explicitly non-dualist, with consciousness emerging
  from physical processes. Reusing c66 would misrepresent the view.
- Theory lists ["c97", "c98"]; c96 inherited.
- Family: Materialism — "conscious experience emerges from physical processes
  without dualism" (page's words).
- Judgment call: the Buddhist "Feeling Self / empty of essence" material is
  folded into the co-emergence framing rather than a separate claim.

### 60 — Chalmers's double-aspect theory of information
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/chalmers-double-aspect-theory-of-information
- **c99 "Information is fundamental"** — information as ontologically
  fundamental, part of the basic furniture of reality. Standalone root.
- **c100 "Information's two faces"** — every information space has a physical
  aspect and a phenomenal aspect: the same information, two expressions.
  Entails **c99**.
- **c101 "Information implies experience"** — "where there is information,
  there is a potential for conscious experience." Entails **c100**.
- Reused **c34** ("consciousness irreducible"): the double-aspect theory is
  articulated *with* naturalistic dualism — "consciousness is not reducible to
  physical processes, yet it is not outside the natural world" — and the page
  states naturalistic dualism must be true for the double-aspect theory to
  hold. Listed directly (most-specific; nothing listed is more specific).
- Theory lists ["c101", "c34"]; c100/c99 inherited via c101.
- Family: Neutral monism — the page: a "double-aspect ontology, reflecting
  ideas from Spinoza to Bertrand Russell's neutral monism."
- Judgment call: c34's inclusion is the one interpretive stretch — the theory
  *presupposes* rather than *asserts* irreducibility — but the page's wording
  ("must be true for the double-aspect theory to hold") justifies listing it.
