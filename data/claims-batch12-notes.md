## Per-theory sources and claims

### 104 — Mandik's qualia quietism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/mandik-s-qualia-quietism
- **c246 "Qualia talk is empty"** — the signature move: the word 'qualia' is
  chaotic and mutually circular; there is no informative position to take
  either way on whether 'qualia' exist. Positive rendering of a neither-
  affirm-nor-deny stance (the DAG's positive-claim discipline). Standalone.
- Reuses **c108** ("Phenomenal character is representational content",
  entails c107): Mandik's intentionalism — "conscious states are
  representational states whose phenomenal character is exhausted by their
  intentional content" (Mandik, 2005).
- Reuses **c228** ("States represent themselves"): Mandik's
  self-representationalism — "what makes an experience phenomenally conscious
  is that it includes, as part of its content, a representation of itself,"
  built into the state, not a separate introspective act (Kriegel-compatible
  structure).
- Deliberately does NOT entail **c0**: Mandik explicitly says "I'm not a
  physicalist" and claims not to assume physicalism.
- Deliberately does NOT entail **c53**: the page stresses he takes neither the
  realist nor the eliminativist side — qualia realism *without qualia*.
- Theory lists ["c246", "c108", "c228"] (c107 inherited).
- Family: Materialism, with caveat (CTT landscape label, not fit).

### 105 — Irvine's scientific eliminativism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/irvine-s-scientific-eliminativism
- **c247 "Consciousness is no natural kind"** — 'consciousness' is a cluster
  concept (like 'game' or 'emotion'), not one unified phenomenon; no single
  natural kind like water or gold. Standalone.
- **c248 "Science should drop 'consciousness'"** — the program: replace the
  word with measurable, operationally defined capacities; work out what the
  measures track in the brain. Entails **c247**.
- **c249 "Introspection can't be the gold standard"** — reports of experience
  are inconsistent and swayed by non-conscious factors; introspection is not
  a reliable measure. Standalone — deliberately NOT routed through c245/c239
  → c53: Irvine's point is methodological unreliability, not that
  phenomenality is fabricated. She never says phenomenal properties are
  illusory.
- No c0: she makes no physics thesis; her eliminativism is about the concept's
  scientific utility, not about what physics covers.
- Theory lists ["c248", "c249"] (c247 inherited).
- Family: Materialism, with caveat: methodological, not metaphysical,
  eliminativism.

### 106 — Blums' conscious Turing machine
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/blums-conscious-turing-machine
  (summary verified by Lenore Blum, 2025-07-06)
- **c250 "Subjective experience is computation"** — the Hard Problem reduces
  to showing subjective experience is computational; a Turing-like machine
  with sensors, actuators, and world-models it takes for the world would be
  conscious — machine consciousness possible, even inevitable. Entails **c42**
  ("Same mind, different hardware", → c41).
- **c251 "Awareness is synchronized inspection"** — the phenomenal mechanism:
  conscious awareness (subjective experience) arises when all processors
  simultaneously inspect a globally broadcast chunk; the synchronized
  unpacking evokes a unique experience whose intensity/valence come from the
  chunk's weight (Axiom A). Entails **c43** ("Consciousness is global
  broadcast"), the Baars-lineage parent — CTM is explicitly built on the
  global-workspace theater model.
- Theory lists ["c250", "c251"] (c42, c43, c41 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Consciousness is a computation: a machine with
  sensors, a world-model it takes for the world, and synchronized inspection
  of broadcasts would feel things."

### 107 — Dennett's multiple drafts
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/dennett-s-multiple-drafts-model
- **c252 "Consciousness is competing drafts"** — the signature model: no
  single stream; parallel multitrack drafts, continuously edited, compete
  for attention; the winner occupies the conscious moment ("fame in the
  brain"). Entails **c0** (Dennett's evolutionary naturalism/materialism is
  explicit on the page: "materialism's most inventive systematic defender").
- **c253 "No inner theater"** — no Cartesian Theater, no inner observer, no
  central headquarters; the self is a "center of narrative gravity." Standalone.
- The page's qualia-as-user-illusions takeaway is NOT added: that is theory
  27's (Dennett's illusionism) core c54, and adding it here would make 107 and
  27 near-indistinguishable. Kept separate per "refine later."
- Theory lists ["c252", "c253"] (c0 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "There's no inner movie screen — many rough
  drafts of experience compete, and the winner gets the spotlight for a moment."

### 108 — Minsky's society of mind
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/minsky-s-society-of-mind
- **c254 "Mind is a society of agents"** — mind built step by step from the
  nonconscious interactions of simple mindless 'agents', organized in layers
  into semi-independent modules; no part knows everything; no central self.
  The "software, not transistors" anti-reductionism supports the functional
  entailment: agents are defined by their roles. Entails **c41** ("Mind is
  defined by its role") and **c0** ("physically locatable modules"; CTT files
  Materialism).
- Theory lists ["c254"] (c41, c0 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Your mind is a society of mindless specialists:
  simple agents, working in layers, that add up to thought — with nobody in
  charge."

### 109 — Agüera y Arcas's computational functionalism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/agueera-y-arcas-s-computational-functionalism
- **c255 "Consciousness is computation"** — consciousness is simply an emergent
  function of the right kinds of computations; life is a self-constructing
  computational process; no new physics needed. Entails **c0**, **c41**
  (functions defined by what they do, not substrates — the page is explicit).
  NOT c43: unlike the Blums, this theory makes no global-broadcast claim.
- **c256 "Theory of mind makes consciousness"** — the distinguishing thesis:
  the social 'arms race' that drove theory of mind, turned on oneself,
  produced self-reflection and the unified self. Entails **c255**. (The page
  name-checks Hofstadter's strange loops and Graziano's AST as influences;
  folded into text, not edges.)
- The coarse-grained-reality-as-computation argument (consciousness "as real
  as temperature") is folded into c255's text/plain, not a separate claim.
- Theory lists ["c256"] (c255, c0, c41 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Consciousness is what computation does when
  life gets complex — and it grew out of minds modeling other minds."

### 110 — Hofstadter's strange loops
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/hofstadter-s-strange-loops
- **c257 "The self is a strange loop"** — consciousness and the self emerge
  from abstract, level-crossing feedback loops: the brain representing
  itself, recursively representing its own representations; the 'I' is a
  dynamic, self-inventing pattern, not a substance. Entails **c0**
  ("consciousness can emerge out of a formal system of firing neurons").
  Deliberately does NOT reuse c228 ("States represent themselves"): c228 is
  Kriegel's per-state self-representation criterion; Hofstadter's loop is a
  system-level recursive self-model — structurally different, don't force it.
- **c258 "Meanings move neurons"** — the controversial core: emergent
  self-referential meanings (the 'I') exert real downward causal potency over
  their own lower-level components. Entails **c257** (the loop is what pushes)
  and reuses **c40** ("Top-down causation is real") as the general parent.
- Theory lists ["c258"] (c257, c40, c0 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "You are a self-referential loop: a system that
  represents itself representing itself — and that pattern can push your
  neurons around."

### 111 — Extended mind (Clark & Chalmers)
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/a-clark-s-extended-mind
- **c259 "Tools can join the mind"** — the parity/coupled-system thesis: when
  brain and external entity are locked in two-way causal interaction they form
  a single coupled cognitive system; the external part counts as part of the
  mind. Entails **c49** ("Mind can extend beyond the skull"), reused — its
  text ("larger cognitive circuits that include the body, tools, and
  environment") genuinely matches.
- Deliberately does NOT route through c5 ("Perception is an interface"):
  Hoffman's desktop metaphor is a different thesis — perception as a
  species-specific survival interface says nothing about external cognitive
  coupling. Also not c85 ("Experience is where it seems"): about phenomenal
  location, not cognitive extension.
- Theory lists ["c259"] (c49 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Thinking doesn't stop at the skull — when brain
  and tools couple up, the tools are part of the mind."

### 112 — Thompson's mind in life
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/thompson-s-mind-in-life
- **c260 "Mind is more than brain"** — mental life is bodily life situated in
  the world, ramifying through body and environment; not reducible to brain
  processes inside the head. Entails **c49**. (Same parent as 111, different
  child — acceptable per "identical claims acceptable initially," but the
  claims differ: Thompson's is about bodily life, 111's about tool coupling.)
- **c261 "Life and mind are continuous"** — the signature thesis: "where there
  is life there is mind"; the self-producing (autopoietic) organization of
  living systems already implies cognition; mind is life's self-organization
  enriched. Entails **c260**.
- The neurophenomenology program (lived experience guiding science) is folded
  into c261's text/plain rather than made a methodological claim — kept the
  count tight.
- Theory lists ["c261"] (c260, c49 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Life and mind are one continuous story: mind is
  what living, self-organizing bodies do in the world — not brain software."

### 113 — Noë's "out of our heads"
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/noe-s-out-of-our-heads-theory
- **c262 "Consciousness is skilled activity"** — consciousness is not a brain
  product but a kind of skilled activity (like dancing): it arises when
  sentient creatures actively engage the world; "we are not our brains."
  Entails **c49**.
- Deliberately does NOT list **c113** ("No brain in a vat"): c113's absolute
  claim — a vat brain *could not* be conscious — is stronger than Noë's stated
  position. His dream evidence (dreams have reduced density/detail but are
  experienced) suggests experience can occur with reduced world-engagement.
  His firm commitment is that brain events *alone* are insufficient, which is
  what c262 states.
- Also not c5/c85 for the same reasons as 111. c112 ("Perception is a bodily
  skill") was considered — Noë agrees perception is skill-like — but c262's
  scope is consciousness generally and the enactive lineage is already captured
  through c49; left for a later pass if a dedicated Noë-on-perception claim is
  wanted.
- Theory lists ["c262"] (c49 inherited).
- Family: Materialism, with caveat: Noë attacks materialism as well as
  dualism; filed per CTT landscape convention.

## Judgment calls and flags for merge review

1. **c250 vs. c255 ("Subjective experience is computation" / "Consciousness
   is computation").** These are genuinely similar — both say experience is
   computational. They differ in lineage: c250 entails c42 (machine
   realizability, inevitable machine consciousness) while c255 entails
   c0+c41 (emergent-from-life computation, no new physics). Kept separate so
   each theory's distinctive edges are honest; a future pass may merge them
   under one parent.

2. **Mandik's c0 and c53.** Both deliberately absent: Mandik rejects standard
   physicalism and refuses the qualia realist/eliminativist fork. If a
   reviewer feels his neurofunctionalism smuggles in c0 anyway, that is a
   content-meaning change to confirm — the page quotes him saying "I'm not a
   physicalist," so the draft honors it.

3. **Dennett 107 vs. theory 27.** Theory 27 (Dennett's illusionism) owns c54;
   107 (multiple drafts) owns c252/c253 and deliberately does not list c54,
   even though the 107 page mentions qualia-as-user-illusions. The
   discrimination is intentional (mechanism vs. anti-realism about qualia)
   but the reviewer should confirm 107 isn't under-claimed.

4. **c113 as Noë's outer bound.** Noë is an enactive externalist, so a reader
   may expect c113 ("No brain in a vat") for 113. The draft deliberately
   withholds it (see 113 notes). If the reviewer reads Noë as committed to
   zero consciousness in a vat brain, add c113 to the claim list — that would
   also inherit c112 ("Perception is a bodily skill"), which Noë would endorse.

5. **Hofstadter c258 → c40.** c40's text ("Higher levels of organization...
   exert real causal force on lower physical levels") is generic enough that
   downward causality reuses it; c258 keeps the strange-loop specificity.
   Confirm no objection to treating c40 as the general parent.

6. **No c43 for Agüera y Arcas.** Unlike the Blums, his page makes no
   global-broadcast claim, so c251/c43 are not entailed despite both theories
   being computational. Intentional.

7. **Extended-mind cluster shape.** c259 (111), c260 (112), c262 (113) are
   three distinct children of c49 — tool coupling, bodily life, skilled
   activity — rather than one shared claim. This keeps the three distinguishable;
   acceptable per standing direction.

8. **c246's positive-claim discipline.** Quietism is a neither-affirm-nor-deny
   stance rendered as a positive claim *about the term 'qualia'* ("the word has
   no stable content; there's nothing informative to decide"). Quiz-wise,
   affirming c246 rules out nothing — that is intended: quietism is compatible
   with both realism- and eliminativism-flavored positions in the graph.
