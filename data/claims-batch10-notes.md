### 84 — Edelman's neural Darwinism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/edelman-s-neural-darwinism-and-reentrant-neural-circuitry
- **c201 "The brain is a selectional system"** — the founding anti-computational
  move: vast variant circuits generated epigenetically, experience selects via
  synaptic strengthening. Entails **c0** (ordinary physics is enough):
  biological naturalism all the way down.
- **c202 "Reentrant signaling weaves the dynamic core"** — massively parallel
  reciprocal signaling, especially thalamus–cortex, integrates distributed
  areas into unitary metastable states; Edelman's answer to the binding
  problem. Entails **c0**.
- **c203 "Qualia are the brain's discriminations"** — "qualia are those
  discriminations"; differences in qualia = differences in neural structure
  and dynamics. Entails **c0**.
- **c204 "Primary consciousness needs no language"** — the remembered present
  requires perceptual categorization + reentry + value memory, but no
  language, narrative, or self; higher-order consciousness is a later,
  separate achievement. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c201", "c202", "c203", "c204"] (all most-specific; c0
  inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "The brain evolves like Darwin's nature:
  neural circuits compete, experience selects the winners; reentrant
  signaling weaves them into conscious scenes."
- Judgment call: Edelman's "hard problem is ill-posed" move is captured
  implicitly by the materialism entailments rather than as its own claim —
  it is a meta-stance, not a consciousness claim, and its positive content
  (mechanism suffices) is already in c201–c203.

### 85 — Hawkins's thousand brains
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/hawkins-s-thousand-brain-remembered-modeling
- **c205 "Thousands of cortical columns vote"** — ~150,000 columns, each with
  complete models via sensorimotor reference frames; unified perception is
  column consensus, no central processor. Entails **c0**.
- **c206 "Awareness is remembered model states"** — awareness = continuous
  memory of the brain's own recent model states; the universal criterion
  (model + store + recall = conscious). Standalone.
- **c207 "The right machine would be conscious"** — functional architecture
  suffices; a Thousand-Brains machine would be conscious but affectless.
  Entails **c206**.
- Theory lists ["c205", "c207"] (c206 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "The cortex runs thousands of little brains
  at once, each modeling the world; perception is what they agree on by vote."
- Judgment call: Hawkins's hard-problem dissolution ("will dissolve rather
  than be solved") is left out as a prediction about the field, not a
  consciousness claim.

### 86 — Merker's midbrain-centered consciousness
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/merker-s-midbrain-centered-reality-space
- **c111 reuse** ("Brainstem, not cortex, is the seat", from batch 5): the
  anatomical claim matches Merker's exactly — upper-brainstem system suffices,
  cortex enriches but does not constitute the conscious field. Deliberate
  reuse; the decortication evidence is the same evidence.
- **c208 "Consciousness is an analog control space"** — Merker's distinctive
  contribution: the selection triangle (target/action/motivation) implemented
  as an analog simulation of body-in-world around an implicit ego-center, and
  the simulation's *structure* is what makes it a conscious mode of function.
  Standalone.
- **c209 "Being conscious needs no self"** — reflective self-awareness is a
  luxury; basic experience needs no self-model. Standalone. (Structural
  cousin of c204's no-language thesis; kept separate since c204 is about
  primary-vs-higher-order evolution while c209 is about the architectonic
  requirements of any consciousness.)
- Theory lists ["c111", "c208", "c209"].
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "No cortex needed: the ancient midbrain runs
  a simulated world around an ego-center — that's the seat of basic
  experience."

### 87 — Ward–Min thalamic dynamic core / gating
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/wards-thalamic-dynamic-core-and-mins-thalamic-reticular-gate
- **c210 "The thalamus generates experience"** — synchronized activity in
  dorsal-thalamic neurons (Ward) / TRN gating of thalamocortical loops (Min);
  cortex computes, thalamus displays and thereby experiences. Entails **c0**.
- **c211 "We experience results, never processes"** — Ward's first evidential
  pillar: no introspective access to computation, only to its results.
  Standalone (evidence for the program, not an entailment).
- Theory lists ["c210", "c211"].
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Consciousness is generated in the thalamus —
  the cortex computes, the thalamus experiences."
- Judgment call: Ward and Min *disagree* on firing mode (steady vs burst as
  the conscious gate) — this is a genuine internal contradiction, but under
  the alignment-not-consistency model the shared positive claims stand and
  the mode dispute is below claim granularity. The notes record it for
  possible future splitting.

### 88 — Dendritic Integration Theory
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/dendritic-integration-theory
- **c212 "Apical dendrites gate awareness"** — nonlinear integration in L5
  apical dendrites; coincidence of top-down and bottom-up streams (calcium
  spikes) gates conscious perception. Entails **c0**.
- **c213 "Breaking dendrite–soma coupling breaks consciousness"** —
  anesthesia disrupts dendritic-somatic coupling, blocking consciousness
  without silencing the brain. Entails **c212**.
- Theory lists ["c213"] (c212, c0 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "The decisive events happen inside single
  neurons' branch-tips: top-down meets bottom-up in apical dendrites, and
  that coincidence opens the gate to awareness."

### 89 — Morsella's passive frame
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/morsella-s-passive-frame-for-action-conflict-resolution
- **c214 "Consciousness resolves action conflicts"** — the common field where
  encapsulated systems with incompatible action tendencies jointly constrain
  skeletal-muscle output. Entails **c0**.
- **c215 "The conscious field is passive"** — the field never reasons, plans,
  initiates, or controls; everything is done unconsciously; its power is
  architectural (simultaneous availability). Standalone.
- **c216 "Phenomenal states are action urges"** — contents are inclinations
  and urges bound to action plans, not detached sensory qualities. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c214", "c215", "c216"].
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Consciousness is a shared stage where warring
  action-plans meet — it never decides anything, but without it your body
  couldn't act on conflicting urges."
- Judgment call: Morsella's "free will does not exist" consequence is left
  out — a negative corollary of passivity, not a consciousness claim.

### 90 — Prinz's attended intermediate-level representation
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/prinz-s-neurofunctionalism-attention-engenders-experience
- **c217 "Attention engenders experience"** — only attention's
  information-flow change generates consciousness; nothing unattended is
  experienced. Entails **c44** ("Attention gates consciousness"): generating
  strictly implies the prerequisite. Deliberate edge into the existing graph —
  Prinz is the strongest version of the attention thesis.
- **c218 "Consciousness lives at the intermediate level"** — intermediate
  perceptual processing: integrated, point-of-view-retaining representations;
  neither raw sensation nor abstract thought. Standalone.
- **c219 "All consciousness is perceptual"** — no experience of thoughts,
  motor commands, or a conscious self. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c217", "c218", "c219"].
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Experience happens where attention meets
  mid-level vision: attention hands a point-of-view picture to working memory
  — and that's what consciousness is."
- Judgment call: c44 was deliberately NOT reused as the listed claim —
  "prerequisite" vs "generator" differ in strength, and Prinz's page leads
  with the stronger thesis. The entailment edge captures the relationship.

### 91 — Bunge's emergent materialism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/bunge-s-emergent-materialism
- **c220 "Mental states are plastic brain states"** — mental states are exactly
  those brain states exhibiting neural plasticity, especially learning; no
  separate mental entity. Standalone — deliberately NOT entailing **c0**:
  Bunge is a materialist but not a physicalist ("physics can explain neither
  life nor mind"), so c0's "ordinary physics is enough" would misrepresent him.
  A caveat records this.
- **c221 "Emergence is real but material"** — the mental is an emergent
  property of the nervous system: a whole-system property not in the
  components, ontologically reducible to matter without epistemological
  reduction to physics. Entails **c220**.
- Theory lists ["c220"] (c221 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "No soul, no mystery-stuff: mind is what a
  learning brain does — an emergent but wholly material property of neural
  systems."
- Judgment call: c39 ("Physical yet irreducible", entails c34) was
  deliberately NOT reused — "wholly physical" clashes with Bunge's explicit
  not-physicalism. The graph keeps them as cousins; a future refinement could
  split c39 into physical/emergent variants.

### 92 — Hobbes's mechanical material mind
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/hobbes-s-mechanical-phantasms-and-the-material-mind
- **c222 "Sensation is motion in matter"** — all mental life is motions of
  matter in an embodied organism; the felt phantasm is the body's outward
  endeavor meeting inward pressure; no immaterial soul. Entails **c0**.
- **c223 "Qualities live in the perceiver"** — colors, sounds, pains are not
  in objects (there: only motions) but wholly in the sentient body. Entails
  **c222**.
- **c224 "Imagination and memory are decaying sense"** — imagination =
  motion persisting after its cause; memory = that motion noticed as past;
  dreams and trains of thought are sequences of such motions. Entails **c222**.
- Theory lists ["c223", "c224"] (c222, c0 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "The first modern materialist: everything
  mental is matter in motion — colors and pains are motions in you, not
  features of the world."
- Caveat: whether Hobbes strictly identifies the phantasm with bodily motion
  or treats it as a mode of the perceiver is contested in scholarship
  (Leijenhorst phenomenalist reading vs identity reading).

### 93 — Armstrong's higher-order perception
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/armstrong-s-higher-order-perception-theory
- **c225 "An inner sense scans the mind"** — introspective consciousness is a
  perception-like inner sense: one neural process scanning another; the
  scanned state becomes introspectively conscious. Entails **c0** (Armstrong's
  materialism — both levels are neural).
- **c226 "Mental states can go unscanned"** — perception, intention, and
  purposive action proceed without introspective awareness (the truck driver);
  consciousness is a relational property, not an intrinsic glow. Entails
  **c225**.
- **c227 "The scanner needn't be conscious"** — no infinite regress: the
  monitoring state need not itself be conscious. Entails **c225**.
- Theory lists ["c225"] (c226, c227, c0 inherited).
- Family: Materialism. Blurb: "Part of your brain watches the rest of it: a
  first-order state becomes conscious when scanned by a perception-like
  inner sense."
- Caveats recorded: (1) Armstrong explains *introspective* consciousness —
  whether the scanner generates phenomenal consciousness or only makes an
  already-phenomenal state available is disputed, and his own texts point
  both ways. (2) Non-reuse discipline: c45 ("Consciousness needs a higher
  thought") was deliberately NOT reused — it is specifically about *thought*,
  and Armstrong's theory is explicitly contrasted with higher-order thought
  accounts (Rosenthal); inner *perception* is a different mechanism.
