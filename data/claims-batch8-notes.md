### 64 — Kadić's monadic panpsychism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/monadic-panpsychism
- **c154 "Subjects don't combine"** — the signature move: subject-summing is
  "demonstrably incoherent" (Coleman 2014), so monadic panpsychism "avoids
  talk of combination" entirely; no amalgamation necessitates a new subject.
  Standalone.
- **c155 "Simples have bare perspective"** — microphenomenal structuralism
  "admits of the basic consciousness of microsubjects-in-isolation": a
  physical ultimate bearing a "point of view," following Tye's split between
  consciousness-as-such (sharp) and representational consciousness (vague).
  Entails **c25** (intrinsic nature is (proto-)conscious).
- **c156 "Phenomenal qualities are relational"** — phenomenal qualities are
  relational "at the level of fundamental simples"; rich experience is
  constituted by relations between microsubjects in a causal structure.
  Standalone (does not entail c155 — relational richness and intrinsic bare
  perspective are distinct theses, and Kadić stresses both).
- Theory lists ["c154", "c155", "c156"]. No caveat.
- Judgment call: c155 does NOT reuse Tye's c150/c151 — Kadić follows Tye's
  consciousness-as-such vs. representational distinction, but c155 is about
  the bare perspective of fundamental simples, not the on/off sharpness of
  phenomenal consciousness or the functional-combination mechanism.

### 65 — Schneider–Bailey entangled superpsychism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/schneider-and-bailey-s-entangled-superpsychism
- **c157 "Deepest level is superconscious"** — the fundamental level hosts
  "superconsciousness": more coherent, more integrated, more advanced
  consciousness than spacetime occupants exhibit. Standalone.
- **c158 "Entangled megaobject is superconscious"** — the holistically
  entangled, non-spatiotemporal "megaobject" possesses "maximal coherence,
  zero entropy" and "the highest form of conscious resonance." Entails c157.
- **c159 "Spacetime emerges from prototime"** — spacetime emerges from
  "prototime," a more fundamental aspatial/quasi-temporal dimension in
  which entanglement keeps its coherence. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c158", "c159"] (c157 inherited). Family: Panpsychism (the
  CTT page's own category).
- Judgment call: c157 does NOT entail c28 ("conscious cosmos first") — the
  page explicitly distinguishes superpsychism from cosmopsychism: the highest
  consciousness is not the whole universe but the non-spatiotemporal
  megaobject. The authors also stress it would be premature to conclude we
  are in a cosmic mind or God's mind — noted, but no theory caveat was
  needed since the claims already avoid that reading.

### 66 — Gambini–Pullin quantum panprotopsychism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/gambini-and-pullin-s-quantum-panprotopsychism
- **c160 "Quantum events have inner feeling"** — an ontology of quantum
  states and events "with internal phenomenal aspects." Entails **c25**.
- **c161 "Entanglement dissolves the combination problem"** — the
  subject-summing problem arises from "implicit hypotheses based on
  classical physics"; at the quantum level, indeterminacy and an
  "exponential number of emergent properties" make the problem dissolve.
  Standalone.
- **c162 "Classical physics has no room"** — "Classical mechanistic
  determinism limits everything ... and leaves no room for novelty or any
  intrinsic aspect that is not epiphenomenal"; the move to quantum
  probabilistic determinism follows from taking consciousness seriously.
  Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c160", "c161", "c162"]. Family: Panpsychism (the CTT page
  itself notes the theory "could have been classified under Panpsychisms";
  it sits in Quantum & Dimensions only because its ontology is quantum).
- Judgment call: c160 does NOT reuse Orch OR's c114–c116 — collapse-by
  microtubule mechanism is a specific physical story; Gambini–Pullin is an
  interpretation-free kinematics-based panprotopsychism. Also distinct from
  c162's move, which is a methodological claim, not a mechanism.

### 67 — Reber's cellular basis of consciousness
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/reber-s-cellular-basis-of-consciousness
- **c163 "Life is sentient from start"** — "sentience emerged with life
  itself"; "the most primitive unicellular species of bacteria are
  conscious, though it is a sentience of a primitive kind." Entails c165.
- **c164 "Minds can't be computed"** — mental states are "intrinsically
  hardware dependent"; "minds are [not] computational and can[not] be
  captured by an artificial intelligence"; this undermines functionalism.
  Standalone.
- **c165 "All experience is consciousness"** — "All experience is mental.
  All organisms that experience have minds, all have consciousness."
  Standalone (the positive reformulation of the hard problem: how all
  experience is consciousness).
- Theory lists ["c163", "c164"] (c165 inherited). Family: Panpsychism (the
  quiz's own category for this theory; the CTT page files it under
  Materialism/Phylogenetic, but the claim set is a bio-panpsychism).
- Judgment call: c163 does NOT entail c25 — Reber is a biological
  reductionist about organic form, not a claim about the intrinsic nature
  of all matter; rocks are not conscious on this view.

### 68 — Wigner–von Neumann consciousness-collapse
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/wigner-von-neumann-s-consciousness-collapse-experience-at-the-quantum-cut
  (matches the THEORY_LINKS_120 entry for id 68 exactly)
- **c166 "Consciousness ends the quantum chain"** — the chain from measured
  system through apparatus, sense organ, and brain "terminates only when a
  conscious observer has an experience"; no purely physical stopping point
  is privileged. Entails **c168**.
- **c167 "Experience is never superposed"** — "experience is invariably
  determinate and never superposed"; this phenomenal definiteness is the
  theory's crucial datum. Standalone.
- **c168 "Consciousness acts on physics"** — Wigner's reciprocal-action
  principle: physics manifestly acts on consciousness (perception), so
  consciousness must act on physics; the collapse is the causal opening.
  Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c166", "c167"] (c168 inherited). Family: Quantum.
- Caveat: Wigner later retreated (decoherence; Ballentine 2019), and recent
  scholarship argues the "consciousness causes collapse" reading of von
  Neumann is overstated — he supplied the two-process formalism and the
  movable cut but ascribed no causal power to the "abstract ego." The
  caveat records both.

### 69 — Stapp's quantum collapse via questions
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/stapp-s-collapsing-the-wave-function-via-asking-questions
- **c169 "Questions collapse the wave function"** — conscious acts of
  attending — "asking questions" of nature — collapse the wave function;
  the quantum Zeno effect gives attention a causal role in the brain.
  Entails **c166** (Stapp is explicitly a von Neumann–Wigner development).
- **c170 "Physics doesn't pick the question"** — "there is nothing in
  quantum mechanics that determines what decides the questions"; the
  choice of the question is "not determined by the laws as we know them" —
  a critical causal gap filled by conscious choice. Standalone.
- **c171 "Classical physics makes mind idle"** — under classical physics
  "there's nothing for consciousness to do ... so it must be some sort of
  an illusion"; mind would be "a passive observer of the scene in which it
  has no function." Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c169", "c170", "c171"] (c166, c168 inherited). No caveat.
- Judgment call: David Wallace's critique (dualism/panpsychism tension in
  collapse views) is recorded in the CTT page but is an objection to the
  theory, not one of its claims — left out of the data per the
  positive-claims rule.

### 70 — Neven's superposition formation
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/nevens-quantum-superposition-formation

  inversion of Penrose: formation, not collapse. Standalone.

  Entails c172.

  phenomenal experience. Standalone.
- **c175 "Classical computers can't be conscious"** — "Turing machines have
  become intelligent but may never become conscious"; sentience requires
  "a quantum Turing machine." Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c173", "c174", "c175"] (c172 inherited via c173). No caveat.
- Note for merge review: c172 is a genuine contrast pair with c166/c169
  (formation vs. collapse as the conscious moment) — fine under the
  alignment-not-consistency model.

### 71 — Chalmers–McQueen quantum collapse
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/chalmers-and-mcqueen-s-quantum-collapse
- **c176 "Collapse needs a consciousness measure"** — a precise theory
  requires "a quantitative account of consciousness" and "a precise collapse
  dynamics" — e.g., IIT's Φ combined with continuous spontaneous
  localization, collapse rate set by the difference in integrated
  information. Standalone.
- **c177 "Quantum collapse is consciousness's gap"** — "wave function
  collapse in quantum mechanics appears to be precisely such a gap" in
  causal closure that non-physical consciousness might fill — "at least not
  obviously ruled out by known physics." Entails **c166**.
- Theory lists ["c176", "c177"] (c166, c168 inherited). No caveat.
- Judgment call: c177 does NOT reuse c144 (Goswami's collapse-by-
  consciousness) — Goswami's is a metaphysical idealism; Chalmers–McQueen
  is a neutral research program compatible with materialism and dualism.

### 72 — Bohm's implicate order
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/bohm-s-implicate-explicate-order
- **c178 "Everything enfolds in implicate order"** — the implicate order is
  "enfolded" order underlying the explicate order of common experience;
  "Everything emerges from and returns to the Whole" via the universal
  flux of folding/unfolding. Standalone.
- **c179 "Mind and matter share ground"** — "the more comprehensive,
  deeper, and more inward actuality is neither mind nor body but rather a
  yet higher-dimensional actuality, which is their common ground"; mind and
  body are related projections, not causal interactors. Entails c178.
- **c180 "Reality is undivided wholeness"** — "undivided wholeness": the
  subject actively participates with the object; reality is dynamic,
  alive, and open-ended — not a machine of independent parts. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c179", "c180"] (c178 inherited). No caveat.
- Judgment call: c179 does NOT entail c19 (one substance, two aspects) —
  the page stresses the ground is "beyond both" mind and body, a higher-
  dimensional actuality rather than a substance with two aspects. This is
  deliberately kept distinct from Schelling's c128→c19 convergence.

### 73 — Faggin's quantum-information panpsychism
- Page: https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/faggin-s-quantum-information-based-panpsychism
- **c181 "Pure quantum states are conscious"** — "a quantum system that is

- **c182 "Seities are conscious agents"** — "seities" — fundamental

- **c183 "Quantum mind can't be computed"** — free will, creativity, and
  consciousness are "non-algorithmic properties that can only exist in a
  fundamental layer of the universe ruled by quantum physics"; no machine
  can ever have or create them. Standalone.
- Theory lists ["c182", "c183"] (c181 inherited). Family: Quantum (the
  quiz's own category for this theory).
- Judgment call: c183 does NOT reuse Orch OR's c114 — c114's
  noncomputationality is tied to quantum-gravity microtubule mechanism;
  Faggin's is about quantum information/privacy and non-algorithmic
  creativity generally. Also note: the CTT page raises post-mortem survival
  and "spiritual beings" framing — theological/eschatological flavor left
  out per the positive-claims rule (cf. batch-5 Aurobindo handling).
